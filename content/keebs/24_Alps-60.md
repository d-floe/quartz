## ALPS 60 Experimental Build

- First built: December 14th, 2023

### Summary

This build was an experiment in attempting to make an ALPS style keyboard using parts I've had on hand.

### Parts

- Case: Walnut Wood
- Configuration: Vinyl shelf liner in lower case
- Keycaps: DCS Alps White on Black
- Mounting: [[Graveshift-65a-Gray-M-O-ring|Graveshift 65a Gray M O-ring]]
- PCB: [[Hineybush-H60|Hineybush H60]]
- Plate: FR4
- Stabilizers: [[TX-Stabilizers-Rev-3|TX Stabilizers Rev.3]]
- Switches: Taihao APC Switches
